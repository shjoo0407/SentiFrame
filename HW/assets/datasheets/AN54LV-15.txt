1. Overall introduction
Raytac’s AN54LV family is a Bluetooth® 6.0 stack (Bluetooth low energy or BLE) modules 
designed based on Nordic nRF54L15 SoC solution, which incorporates: GPIO,  
QSPI (emulated), SPI, UART, I2C, I2S, PDM, PWM, ADC and NFC interfaces for 
connecting peripherals and sensors.
Product Character:  
1. MCU (Microcontroller Unit) featuring a 128 MHz Arm Cortex-M33 processor.
2. Embedded 1524 KB NVM and up to 256 KB RAM.
3. An integrated RISC-V coprocessor.
4. The multiprotocol 2.4 GHz radio modes are supported based on customer preference.
5. Compact size with (L) 8.4 x (W) 6.4 x (H) 1.5mm
6. Wide supply voltage range 1.7V to 3.6V.
7. Operation temperature from -40℃ to 105℃.
8. Up to 32 GPIOs.

1.2. Features
‧Multi-protocol 2.4GHz radio
‧128MHz Arm Cortex-M33 with Thumb-2 (16/32-bit instruction set)

  ‧I2C up to 400 kHz 
‧Three pulse width modulator (PWM) units with EasyDMA

‧ADC with up to eight programmable gain channels.

‧Near field communication (NFC)
