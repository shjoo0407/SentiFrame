Description 
This new generation MOSFET has been designed to minimize the on-
state resistance (R DS(ON)) and yet maintain superior switching 
performance, making it ideal fo r high efficiency power management 
applications. 
 
Applications 
• Load Switch 
• DC-DC Converters 
• Power Management Functions  
 
Features 
• Low On-Resistance 
• ESD Protected Gate 
• Totally Lead-Free & Fully RoHS compliant (Notes 1 & 2) 
• Halogen and Antimony Free. “Green” Device (Note 3) 
• Qualified to AEC-Q101 Standards for High Reliability 
 
Mechanical Data 
• Case: SC59 
• Case Material – Molded Plasti c. UL Flammability Rating 94V-0 
• Moisture Sensitivity:  Level 1 per J-STD-020 
• Terminals: Finish - Matte Tin Solderable per MIL-STD-202, 
Method 208 
• Terminal Connections: See Diagram 
• Weight: 0.014 grams (approximate)
